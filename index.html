04 · Our Approach

The finish is in the details.

Good tailoring is more than stitching fabric together. It is about proportion, comfort, clean finishing and understanding how a garment should sit on you. We keep the process personal: discuss the design directly, decide the details, and get the piece made with care.

Looking for something specific?
Bring a reference photo or your preferred design to the shop. We can discuss what can be created for your fabric and fit.
